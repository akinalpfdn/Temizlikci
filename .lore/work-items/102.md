They look like ordinary folders (not volume roots, not symlinks, distinct inodes) but /.nofollow exposes the whole volume again, so the first startup-disk scan reported 731 GB on a 494 GB disk. An explicit skip list is the only reliable rule; a total above used capacity is the tell-tale sign of a new alias.
