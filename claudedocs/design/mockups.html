Visual direction: a calm, instrument-grade Mac utility — native macOS 26 chrome, a quiet neutral canvas, and the sunburst as the only source of color on screen.

Interactive prototype, not a screenshot. Example data is modeled on the scans of your Mac from 21 September 2026; item names and sizes are illustrative. Colors are the validated palette below; everything else uses macOS system colors read from your Mac.

Decisions to approve

Layout

Sidebar · content · inspector

The sidebar holds places (Locations) and views (Insights), never the folder tree: HIG caps sidebars at two levels. The hierarchy lives in the content area as a breadcrumb, the sunburst, and a list. The inspector shows the selected item and its actions (Option-Command-I).

Color

Color by top-level folder

Each first-ring folder takes a palette slot in size order. Its descendants keep that hue and get lighter the deeper they sit, with 2 px separators between segments. The ninth folder onward joins a neutral “Smaller items” segment. The list shows the same swatches, so color never carries meaning on its own.

Insight

Highlight Reclaimable

A toolbar toggle turns the same chart into a cleanup map. Items that are safe to remove turn green, items that need their own tool turn amber, and everything else fades to gray. Each state also has a label and a symbol, never color alone.

Vocabulary

Three safety levels

Safe to Remove: regenerates by itself. Remove with Tool: Xcode, simctl, or Android Studio removes it. Keep: your data, so no delete action is offered. Each level comes with a one-sentence reason in the inspector.

Interaction

Select, open, go up

Click selects and updates the inspector. Double-click or Return opens the folder. Clicking the center or pressing Escape goes up. The selected item's name, size, and share are always written in the center, so nothing depends on hovering.

Honesty

Explain the gap

“System, snapshots & purgeable” and “Needs access” are hatched segments with an explanation. Moving to the Trash shows an Undo toast and a reminder that space is freed only when the Trash is emptied. No alert for undoable actions; an alert with a specific verb only for irreversible tool deletions.

Chart palette

macOS system colors are tuned for controls, not for adjacent chart segments. Validated against your Mac's real surfaces, they fail: yellow sits outside the lightness band in light mode, and in dark mode green and orange collapse for deuteranopia (ΔE 4.9). The chart therefore uses a custom palette in the same color families, with separate light and dark values. Every other color in the app stays a system color.

App icon concept

Three rings of a sunburst, with one outer segment swept loose and catching the light. It stands for the space you get back. Built as separate layers (background, rings, lifted segment) so Icon Composer can render it as Liquid Glass in the default, dark, clear, and tinted appearances.
